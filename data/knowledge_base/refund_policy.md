# Refund Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to purchases made through the company support portal and subscription services.

## Refund Eligibility
- Refund requests are accepted only within 7 days of the original purchase date.
- If the customer requests a refund after 7 days, the request is not eligible for a refund under standard policy.
- Refunds for annual plans are reviewed case by case and may be denied if the plan was already used for a significant period.

## Repeated Refund Requests
- If a customer submits multiple refund requests for the same order within 90 days, the case should be flagged as a repeat request and escalated to a specialist.
- Repeated refund abuse may trigger a scripted refusal.

## Required Information
To process a refund review, the support team should request the order ID or account email when the customer has not provided it.

## Escalation Guidance
Escalate when:
- the refund request is outside the 7-day window,
- the customer submits a repeat refund request,
- or the policy cannot be verified from the available information.
