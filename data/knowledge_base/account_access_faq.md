# Account Access FAQ

## Common Issues
- Password reset: Customers can use the password reset link on the login page.
- Login problems: If the customer cannot access the account, confirm the email address used for registration.
- Account lockout: If the account is locked after repeated failed attempts, the user should wait 15 minutes before trying again.

## Required Information
Before troubleshooting account access, request one of the following if not already provided:
- the registered email address,
- the account ID,
- or the most recent order ID.

## Escalation Guidance
Escalate to support if:
- the customer cannot verify their account ownership,
- the account is suspected to be compromised,
- or the requested information is missing after one follow-up request.
